The question worth asking

Where does the money actually go?

Here is the published design in plain language. Check real transactions and contract permissions yourself so you can make a smart move.

When a platform holds your balance
  • You deposited, and the operator held the balance
  • Your dashboard showed a number
  • Then withdrawals paused and the site went offline
  • That number turned out to be a promise, not possession
How Bitcoin Wealth is designed to route payments
  • No withdraw button for a routed payment, because it goes to a wallet
  • Activating a slot is designed to split that payment inside the same transaction
  • Each portion is directed to receiving members' own wallets
  • A completed wallet transfer does not depend on an operator approving a later withdrawal
Arithmetic

Where every payout comes from

Worked out from the programme's own numbers, so you can decide with the full picture.

14 in, 14 outFourteen members each pay one slot price into a cycle, and fourteen payments go out. Nothing is created in between.
No outside incomeThe material describes no product, trading or mining. Every unit a member receives was paid in by another member.
About 93% waitingEach new layer is 14 times bigger than the one above it, so most members are always in the newest layer, not yet paid.

1 cycle completes for every 14 newer members who have paid in and are still waiting for their own.
