How to Read This Site

What this page covers

Understand where this site content comes from and what the three labels mean.

This site teaches the Bitcoin Wealth programme using the material published about it: a presentation deck titled Built by the People, For the People, a written explanation of the smart contract, and four wallet setup guides.

Nothing here is invented. Where the material states something, this site states it. Where the material claims something, this site attributes the claim rather than repeating it as fact. And where the numbers in the material can be worked through, this site works through them.

The 3 Labels, Used Throughout

Source fact

Something the material explicitly shows or states. Example: slot 1 costs 0.001 BTC. That is printed in the deck.

Source claim

A promise, projection, or characterisation the material makes. Example: "unlimited earning potential." That is what the deck says. It is not a verified outcome, and this site does not present it as one.

Arithmetic

Arithmetic performed on numbers printed in the material. No outside data, no opinions, just the sums. You can check every one of them yourself with a calculator.

What this site will not do

  • It will not tell you to join.
  • It will not tell you to stay away.
  • It will not invent earnings, testimonials, deadlines, or guarantees.

By the end you should be able to explain how the system works, what it promises, and what has to be true for those promises to pay out. The decision is then yours to make.

Key takeaways

  • Course content comes from the supplied Bitcoin Wealth material, not from outside sources.
  • Source facts, source claims, and arithmetic are labelled separately throughout.
  • The goal is understanding, not persuasion in either direction.
Check it against the source

Everything on this page is drawn from the programme's own presentation. Read the original for yourself rather than taking this site's word for it.
