The Programme in Its Own Words

What this page covers

Summarise how the presentation introduces and positions Bitcoin Wealth.

The presentation opens with a positioning statement and a mission. Here is what it says.

Source claim

Bitcoin Wealth describes itself as more than a platform, calling itself a movement built for the people. It says it was founded by Bitcoin believers who are creating a global ecosystem where communities come together to earn, grow, and succeed in Bitcoin as one. It describes combining decentralised systems with scalable ecosystem expansion to shift power back to individuals.

Source claim

The deck calls the system the KING of Smart Contracts and describes it as a groundbreaking evolution in the smart contract space, introducing a continuous Bitcoin-earning matrix system designed for scalability, automation, and long-term opportunity.

The 3 Ways to Get Bitcoin

The deck frames its offer against two alternatives:

Source claim
  • Mining Bitcoin requires expensive equipment and high electricity costs.
  • Buying Bitcoin is risky, as prices fluctuate and losses can happen.
  • Earning Bitcoin is described as the smartest way to get it, and the deck says Bitcoin Wealth makes it simple, fast, and cost-effective.

Worth noticing. The first two descriptions name a cost and a risk. The third names neither. When you reach The Numbers you will be able to work out what the third option costs and what it depends on, using the deck's own numbers. Hold the question until then.

The 3 Pillars

Source claim
  • A ready-made growth system. The deck describes a fully structured ecosystem where you participate, build your network, and grow with others through a system-driven model.
  • A people-powered network. It says you are not starting alone, and that collective participation drives momentum and shared opportunity.
  • A smart distribution engine. It describes a next-generation smart contract matrix designed for fair, transparent, and continuous reward flow.
Source fact

The deck states an entry point of 0.001 BTC for slot 1, and describes the system as operating since 2024.

Key takeaways

  • Bitcoin Wealth positions itself as a decentralised, community-owned earning system, not a company product.
  • It frames earning as superior to mining and buying, without naming a cost or risk for earning.
  • Entry begins at 0.001 BTC for slot 1.
  • Most of the positioning language is claim, not verifiable fact.
