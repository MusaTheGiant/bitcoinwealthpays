Ranks and Rewards

What this page covers

Know the five rank tiers and what each one requires.

Source fact

The deck lists one qualifier and four rankings.

TierNameTarget
QualifierIgniteSponsor 2 people
Ranking 1BeginnerReach slot 3
Ranking 2LeaderReach slot 6
Ranking 3ChampionReach slot 9
Ranking 4Grand ChampionReach slot 12

Reading the qualifier carefully

Source fact

The entry qualifier is not a payment or a time period. It is sponsoring two people. The deck also states that the spillover system becomes active once the first two members join.

Two is a floor, not a ceiling

Source fact

Two is the minimum needed to qualify for Ignite and switch spillover on. The deck sets no upper limit anywhere. Nothing in the material caps how many people you may personally introduce, and the deck separately states there are no limits on rewards based on ranks.

This matters because "sponsor 2 people" reads like a task you tick off and stop. It is not. Two is the threshold that activates your position. Beyond that you may introduce as many people as you wish, and each one fills a position in your structure rather than counting against some quota.

Practically: two unlocks the system, and everything above two is your own choice about how far to build.

This is worth sitting with. The first thing the programme asks of a new member is to bring in two more members. Everything above that, slots 3, 6, 9 and 12, is reached by your matrix filling, which requires more members again. Recruitment is not an optional extra in this structure. It is the qualifier and the engine.

The 5 Key Advantages

Source claim

The deck describes itself as the world's first Matrix Plan, fully transparent and automated, and lists:

  1. No limits on rewards based on ranks.
  2. No expiration or freeze policy for slots.
  3. Automatic and instant withdrawals.
  4. Limitless earnings through unlimited slot recycling.
  5. Automatic slot upgrades with the quickest spillover mechanism.

What the material does not say. It does not define a time frame for reaching any rank, does not state any reward attached to a rank beyond the slot itself, and does not say what happens to a member whose matrix never fills. Those are three fair questions to ask before deciding anything.

Key takeaways

  • Five tiers: Ignite, Beginner, Leader, Champion, Grand Champion.
  • The entry qualifier is sponsoring two people, not a payment. Two is the minimum, and the deck sets no maximum.
  • Every tier above that is reached by your matrix filling with more members.
  • No time frames, rank bonuses, or non-completion outcomes are specified in the material.
