What Is Bitcoin Wealth?

Bitcoin Wealth is a matrix programme that runs on a smart contract deployed on BNB Smart Chain. Members activate numbered slots, and the contract distributes each payment automatically to other members according to rules written into its code.

That is the short version. Everything below explains it properly, using the programme's own material, with the claims marked as claims and the numbers worked through so you can check them.
