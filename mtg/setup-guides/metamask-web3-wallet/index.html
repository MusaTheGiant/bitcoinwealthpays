How to set up your MetaMask Web3 Wallet

Set up and secure MetaMask, step by step

What you will need
  • A smartphone or a desktop browser
  • Pen and paper for your recovery phrase
  • Two separate safe places to store copies
1

Download from the official site only

Go to metamask.io and download the app or browser extension from there. Never download from an ad, a search result, or a link someone sent you.

2

Create a new wallet

Open MetaMask and choose Create a New Wallet. Accept the terms to begin setup.

3

Set a strong password

Create a password you will remember. This unlocks MetaMask on this device only. It is not your recovery phrase.

4

Write down your 12 secret words

MetaMask reveals your Secret Recovery Phrase: 12 words in exact order. Write them on paper by hand. Never screenshot them.

5

Confirm your phrase

MetaMask asks you to re-enter some of the words to prove you saved them correctly. Enter them in the same order.

6

Add the BNB Smart Chain

In Settings, add the BNB Smart Chain network so your wallet can receive BNB and USDT.

Golden rules: protect your 12 words

  • Anyone who has your 12 words owns everything in your wallet.
  • Never share them with anyone, for any reason, ever.
  • No real support agent will ever ask you for them.
  • Never store them as a screenshot, photo, email, or cloud note.

Write them on paper and keep two copies in separate safe places.
