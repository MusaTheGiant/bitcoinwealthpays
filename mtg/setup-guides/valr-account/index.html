How to set up your VALR Account

Register, verify, fund with Rand and buy BNB

What you will need
  • Your ID document
  • A South African bank account in your own name
  • An email address you control
1

Download the official app

Install the VALR app from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store, or go to valr.com. Never download from an ad or unofficial link.

2

Create your account

Open the app, tap Sign Up, enter your email address, create a strong password and agree to the terms.

3

Verify your identity

Go to Verify, enter your personal details and upload your ID document and a selfie. This keeps your account secure and unlocks full features.

4

Fund your account with Rand

Tap Deposit, select ZAR (South African Rand) and choose EFT or Instant EFT. Follow the instructions to fund your VALR account.

5

Buy BNB

Go to the Markets tab, search for BNB/ZAR, tap Buy, enter the amount of Rand you want to spend and confirm your order.

6

You now have BNB

Your BNB will appear in your VALR wallet. You can hold it, trade it, or withdraw it to your own wallet.

VALR supports local ZAR deposits, which makes it a common starting point in South Africa.
