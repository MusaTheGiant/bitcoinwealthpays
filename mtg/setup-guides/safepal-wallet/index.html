How to set up your SafePal Wallet

Download, secure your seed phrase and hold BTCB, BNB and USDT

What you will need
  • A smartphone
  • Pen and paper for your recovery phrase
  • A safe, private place to store that paper
1

Download the official app

Install SafePal from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store, or go to safepal.com. Never download from an ad, a direct message, or a random link.

2

Create a new software wallet

Open the app, tap Create Wallet, choose Software Wallet, then set a strong password you will remember.

3

Write down your 12 words

SafePal shows you a 12-word seed phrase. Write it on paper in the exact order. Never screenshot it, photograph it, or save it to your phone or cloud.

4

Store it offline and tell no one

Confirm the words to finish setup, then hide the paper somewhere safe. Anyone who asks for your 12 words is a scammer. SafePal will never ask.

5

Add BNB Chain tokens only

Tap the plus icon to manage tokens, filter by BNB Smart Chain, and switch on only BTCB, BNB and USDT (BEP-20) for a clean starter wallet.

6

Receive and test safely

Tap Receive, confirm the network says BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20), then send a small test amount first before moving anything bigger.

Your keys, your crypto. Never share your 12 words with anyone.
