How to set up your Binance Account

Register, verify and fund with Rand, step by step

What you will need
  • Your ID document
  • A bank account in your own name
  • A phone number and email address you control
1

Download the official app

Install Binance from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store, or visit binance.com. Never download from an ad or a link someone sent you.

2

Create your account

Register with your email or phone number and set a strong password you do not use anywhere else.

3

Verify email and phone

Binance sends you a code. Enter it to confirm your email and phone number.

4

Complete identity verification

Submit your ID document and take a selfie. This is required by law before you can deposit or trade.

5

Turn on two-factor security

Enable 2FA in your security settings. This protects your account even if someone learns your password.

6

Deposit your Rand

Go to Deposit, choose ZAR, and follow the bank transfer instructions to fund your account.

Good to know before you start

  • Have your ID document ready before you begin.
  • Your Binance name must match your ID and your bank account exactly.
  • Verification can take a few minutes to a few hours. Be patient.
  • Never share your password or security codes with anyone.
