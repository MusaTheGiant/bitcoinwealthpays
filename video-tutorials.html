Member training

Why this video is on an independent site. It is a training session recorded by a team inside the programme, not by us, and it is here because it answers a question the presentation does not: who actually decides where a new member is seated.

The deck says the contract handles matrix placement automatically. This session teaches a sponsor to choose the branch and position, confirm it in a back office, notify the member and update the matrix record. Both things are worth knowing, and the difference between them is the reason we have kept the video rather than summarised it. The 14 Positions explains what that difference means for you.
